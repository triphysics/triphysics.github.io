What I do

Ten years of turning large, noisy scientific datasets into models and tools that other people rely on, now applied to industrial test and sensor data.

  • Predictive modelling

    Machine-learning and time-series models for failure detection and prediction, validated against reference data, with an honest account of where a model should not be trusted.

    Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Databricks

  • Data pipelines and quality control

    Automated pipelines that run unattended at scale, with built-in checks for anomalies, outliers and failed runs, and structured metadata so every result can be traced.

    Python, SQL, Databricks, AiiDA, Atomate2, Git, Linux

  • LLM and RAG tools

    Retrieval-augmented-generation tools that automate manual, knowledge-intensive analysis steps, so experts spend their time on judgement rather than lookup.

    Python, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation

  • Statistics and communication

    Analysis of multi-factor experimental data, hypothesis testing and uncertainty quantification, explained clearly to scientists, engineers and non-specialists.

    R, Python, statistical modelling, training and workshops

About me

I trained as a physicist, with a PhD in computational materials science from the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore. For the next decade, at Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the University of Antwerp, most of my work was really data work: generating and cleaning large datasets, building pipelines that ran without supervision, checking results against experiments, and writing tools that other researchers could use.

Over the last few years that shifted towards machine learning. I built and validated prediction models, paying close attention to where they fail, and developed LLM and retrieval-augmented-generation tools to automate slow, manual analysis. Since September 2026 I have been a data scientist intern at Sandvik Coromant, building models for failure detection from instrument and sensor data.

I live in Uppsala, have full authorization to work in Sweden, and am learning Swedish.
